## Role actions
Second Wind, Leg Sweep, Bloodbath, Feint, Arm's Length, True North — all ➖.

## Not simulated
- **Chakra from crits.** Deep Meditation and Enhanced Brotherhood open Chakra when the player's hits crit or land, which the sim doesn't roll; only the Meditation actions open it.
